goals: final desired state predicates. Predicates use type=edge (source,relation,target), type=node_attribute (node,attribute,operator,value), type=all_of/any_of (predicates), or type=not (predicate). Use canonical entity names from vocabulary.objects, vocabulary.relations and vocabulary.attributes. Aliases map language to canonical names. State goals describe outcomes, not invented intermediate requirements.

events: explicitly requested interactions not captured by final state. Each is {"action":"native_action_name","bindings":{"$role":"canonical_entity"}}. Use vocabulary.actions for native action names and argument roles. Do not guess a plan or invent preconditions/effects.

ordering: explicitly requested order only, as {"before":"event:0","after":"goal:0"}. References use zero-based indices in goals/events. Mere sentence co-occurrence does not imply causal dependency.

unresolved: unrepresentable clauses, ambiguous objects, unresolved quantities, unknown initial facts or unsupported constraints, with text and reason. Preserve every substantive task requirement in goals/events/ordering or unresolved. Do not silently replace an unknown object with a similar known one. Quantifiers need a known instance inventory; otherwise retain them here.
